For years, frontline teams in care have faced a persistent challenge: ensuring that staff are not just trained, but genuinely ready to take on shifts safely and compliantly. Traditional LMS platforms track course completion, but in reality, completion does not always translate to workforce readiness. This disconnect can create risk and a considerable administrative burden.
