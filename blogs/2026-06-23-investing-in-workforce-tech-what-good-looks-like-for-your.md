For care providers, investing in workforce technology is rarely just about replacing spreadsheets or digitising existing processes. The real opportunity is bigger than that. Good workforce technology should make work feel clearer, fairer and easier for the people using it every day, from frontline employees and managers to regional leaders, finance teams and the board.
